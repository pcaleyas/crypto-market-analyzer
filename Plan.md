### ⚙️ PHASE 2 [COMPLETED]: Feature Engineering and Realistic "Target" (src/features.py)

Here we prepare the data so the model doesn't trick itself.

* **Technical Indicators:** Calculate RSI, MACD, Moving Averages, and Bollinger Bands on the BTC price.
* **Data Unification:** Perform a temporal `merge` (aligning by dates) of the BTC dataframe with the exogenous data (VIX, DXY).
* **Realistic Target:** Instead of predicting any upward movement, we require the upward movement to be profitable after applying the commissions and slippage defined in `config.py`.
* **Target Logic:** `Target = 1` if `(Tomorrow_Close / Today_Close) - 1 > (FEE_RATE * 2 + SLIPPAGE)`. Otherwise, `Target = 0`.
* **Cleaning:** Remove rows with null values generated by the temporal lag of indicators (`df.dropna()`) and save in `processed`.

### 🧠 PHASE 3 [COMPLETED]: Baseline and Walk-Forward Validation (src/train_base.py)

Implementation of the first base models (Random Forest and XGBoost) using time-series appropriate validation methods.

* **Walk-Forward Validation:** Implemented Scikit-Learn's `TimeSeriesSplit` with 5 splits. Train on cumulative time windows and evaluate on the next segment to properly simulate real trading.
* **Pipelines and Scaling:** Integrated `StandardScaler` together with `RandomForestClassifier` and `XGBClassifier` into independent Pipelines.
* **Financial Evaluation:** Prioritized financial metrics like annualized Sharpe Ratio and Max Drawdown (MDD) over raw Accuracy.
* **RF Results:** Average Sharpe of `-0.2896` and MDD of `-28.06%`. The simple Random Forest baseline struggles heavily to beat market friction.
* **XGBoost Results:** Average Sharpe of `0.1979` and MDD of `-33.31%`. XGBoost (with class imbalance adjustment via `scale_pos_weight`) manages to find an edge and beat costs, though drawdowns remain deep due to the lack of pure sequential memory.

### 🚀 PHASE 4 [COMPLETED]: Sequence Modeling (src/train_dl.py)

Development and experimentation with LSTM Neural Networks to capture complex temporal patterns in Sliding Windows.

* **Sliding Windows:** Transformation of tabular data into 3D tensors (14 timesteps) for the neural network.
* **Overfitting Prevention:** Inclusion of `Dropout` layers (30%) and `EarlyStopping` with a patience of 5 epochs monitoring the chronological validation loss (20% of each fold's training set).
* **Experimented Variants:**
  1. **Class Weights:** LSTM with a balanced penalty to balance the signal (Sharpe: `0.0478`, MDD: `-14.42%`).
  2. **Dynamic Threshold:** Optimization of the decision threshold (buy probability) on the validation set of each fold. Proved to be by far the best approach (Sharpe: `0.8965`, MDD: `-39.59%`).
  3. **Regression:** Prediction of continuous returns and thresholding based on commissions (Sharpe: `-0.0071`, MDD: `-31.23%`).

### 📊 PHASE 5 [COMPLETED]: Backtesting Engine (src/backtest.py)

The definitive step between data science and finance. No Machine Learning here, just raw trade simulation.

* **Simulator Integration:** Integrated the `vectorbt` library, which is extremely fast for portfolio calculation based on Pandas tensors.
* **Account Configuration:** Started an "Out-of-Sample" simulation (January 2024 - June 2026) with a $10,000 capital, respecting the 0.15% Fee and 0.05% Slippage per trade.
* **Tangible Results:** The AI detected risk profiles very well, trading only 14 times in over 2 years. It achieved a solid profit (+29.56% and a Sharpe ratio of 0.46) although below the amazing Buy&Hold rally of the same period (+50.07%). Despite the Drawdown being similar to the pure market, the strategy is highly asymmetric, showing massive average profit per trade (+33.7%) vs small controlled losses (-5.2%).
* **Visualization:** Automatic generation of `backtest_report.txt` with all detailed metrics and an interactive HTML equity curve graph.
